Welcome to **ITCH.IO Filter**, a community-made mod designed to give you back control over what you see on the itch.io desktop application.

Let's be honest: browsing the app can sometimes feel like wading through endless waves of AI-generated shovelware, spammy visual novels, and ideological filler. This mod was built to fix that by cleaning up your feed so you can actually find real indie games.

## Key Features

- **Automatic installation:** Uses an intelligent installer that locates your itch.io directory automatically.
- **Built-in filtering:** Blocks AI content, terrible horror visual novels, woke games, and other unwanted noise out of the box.
- **Customizable overlay:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+F` inside the app whenever you want to set your own custom filters.

## How to Get Started

1. Download the installer package.
2. Run the executable and let it detect your itch.io folder automatically.
3. Open your itch.io app and enjoy a cleaner feed.
4. Press `Ctrl+Shift+F` to open the overlay and fine-tune your preferences at any time.

Take control of your discovery feed and enjoy browsing itch.io the way it was meant to be experienced.
